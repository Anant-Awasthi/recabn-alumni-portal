Official College Info

Rajkiya Engineering College, Ambedkar Nagar

Rajkiya Engineering College, Ambedkar Nagar (RECABN) was established in 2010 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to deliver high-quality technical education and foster research excellence.

The institute is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and is affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow (College Code 737). REC Ambedkar Nagar is dedicated to academic development, offering state-of-the-art laboratory facilities and fostering top engineering talent.

The college currently offers B.Tech programs in three core disciplines, with a verified intake of 60 seats per branch:

  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Electrical Engineering (EE)
  • Civil Engineering (CE)

Alumni Initiative Framework

The RECABN Alumni Association (RAA) is an engagement framework designed to organize the graduate community of Rajkiya Engineering College, Ambedkar Nagar. With 12 graduating batches (2014–2025) now established in diverse sectors, this initiative aims to formalize batch records and communication channels.

The association focuses on developing advisory and career-support pathways under college guidance. By establishing a central alumni directory, the network intends to support batch-wide communication, career exchanges, and institutional advisory collaborations.

How can alumni contribute? The framework coordinates strictly qualitative, voluntary advisory support:

  • Mentorship & Advising: Guiding students on career paths, post-graduate study, and corporate expectations.
  • Expert Lectures: Delivering seminars on technical advancements and industrial applications.
  • Career Guidance: Advising on PSU entrance preparations, research applications, and internships.
  • Academic Feedback: Providing industry inputs to assist with curriculum alignment reviews.

Note: The association operates under a draft constitution and bylaws, currently undergoing institutional review to align with the college's administrative guidelines.

Strategic Objective

To establish a formal, verified database of graduates, build communication channels between batchmates, and coordinate collaborative initiatives that benefit students and the college.

Core Vision

To foster a lifelong connection among graduates, promoting mutual growth and contributing to the academic excellence of REC Ambedkar Nagar.

The roadmap for establishing the RECABN Alumni Association follows a phased approach to ensure strict alignment with college administrative policies and sustainable long-term governance.

Regional Network Hubs

To support peer networking and regional student support, the framework structures local nodes in key metropolitan hubs. These chapters will coordinate local meetups and industry-academic interactions in alignment with college guidance:

Noida / NCR Hub

Primary coordination node for corporate networking, startup mentoring, and IT sector linkages across Noida, Gurgaon, and Greater Noida.

Lucknow & Home Node

Centering academic liaison, institutional collaborations, university affairs with AKTU, and campus activities at Ambedkar Nagar.

Bengaluru Tech Chapter

Connecting software developers, tech entrepreneurs, and R&D specialists to support student developer bootcamps and tech internships.

Delhi & Metro Hubs

Focusing on civil services aspirants, public sector undertakings (PSUs), government engineering nodes, and competitive exam guidance.

West-South & Overseas

Reaching out to regional cells in Mumbai, Pune, and Hyderabad, alongside a central Overseas cell for global research and academic links.

The core focus of the RAA network is supporting the academic and career development of current B.Tech students.

Through structured mentorship channels, verified alumni will provide guidance on industry expectations, internship search, and resume reviews. This helps students navigate their transition into professional careers and provides them with direct access to experienced mentors.

Placement & Resume Guidance

Proposed sessions where alumni review resumes and provide feedback on technical expectations in different engineering domains.

Internship Advisory

Industry Trends Lectures

Proposed guest lectures and webinars to keep students informed about developments in Information Technology, Electrical, and Civil engineering.
