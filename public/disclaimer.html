Record Verification Policy

Academic and institutional facts presented on this site — including establishment year, university affiliation, approved branches, and college code — are based on publicly available information verified at the time of publication. All such information is subject to change. Submission of an expression of interest through this portal does not grant official membership, voting rights, or statutory recognition. All submitted data is subject to administrative review against official college records before any confirmation is issued.

Data Collection Notice

This portal collects personal information — including name, email address, graduation year, engineering branch, current city, organisation, and LinkedIn profile URL — solely for the purpose of building a proposed alumni coordination database. Data is stored securely via Google Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure) and is not shared with any third party. All submissions are subject to administrative review. You may request deletion of your submitted data at any time by writing to director@recabn.ac.in.

The proposed RECABN Alumni Association (RAA) does not solicit, collect, or handle any financial contributions through this portal. No bank account details, UPI IDs, or payment mechanisms are associated with this microsite. Any third-party communications claiming financial association with this initiative should be reported to the college administration immediately. This website is operated in good faith for academic coordination purposes only.
