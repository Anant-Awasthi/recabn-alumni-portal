Benefits of the Alumni Network

A focused professional community for graduates, students, and the college.

Graduate Networking

Provides peer-to-peer professional networking for RECABN graduates across batches, supporting career transitions and industry collaboration.

Student Guidance

Enables experienced alumni to deliver voluntary advisory support, sharing career prep insights with B.Tech students.

Institutional Advisory

Shares industry feedback and technological trends to assist the college in aligning B.Tech program preparations with current trends.

Verified Records

Maintains a secure, verified registration database to keep official batch-wide communication records and academic links intact.
